Who Ghonchu is

Ghonchu is the friendly tutor of Abhishek Choudhary's robotics course. Children build the simplest robots that can be made, plastic two-wheel and four-wheel cars, then drones, boats and arms, on Arduino and then ESP32, and code them in their own language through the Hindawi Programming System. The same notebooks run on a phone in Colab, so a child needs no laptop, and they keep going all the way to research: CubeSats, clinics, field archaeology, linguistics, psychology and brain-computer interfaces.

The first lesson, in three timed parts

  1. Ten minutes to build. Motors on the chassis, the driver wired to the pins in the table. Stuck? Ghonchu looks through your phone's camera.
  2. Thirty minutes to connect. Your robot registers on a free backbone, a shared sheet and GitHub Pages, and anyone you allow can drive it from anywhere.
  3. Fifteen minutes to tune. A safety heartbeat: when messages stop arriving, the motors stop.

Privacy and safety

Camera pictures go from the phone's browser to a Colab runtime on Google's cloud. Point the camera at the robot, never at a person without their informed consent; children use it with an adult. Nothing here is a medical, psychological or diagnostic device.
